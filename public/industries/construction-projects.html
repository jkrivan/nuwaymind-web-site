Business reality

The procurement model must reflect the specific pressure points of Construction & Project-Based Businesses.

Project procurement happens under schedule pressure, changing site conditions, subcontractor dependencies and budget constraints. Decisions made quickly on site must still be visible and defensible.

The organisation needs strong links between project budgets, approved suppliers, subcontract scopes, material orders, change orders, delivery confirmation and invoice approval.

Procurement maturity is visible in how well the business prevents margin leakage, controls variations and keeps project teams aligned with commercial commitments.

Main industry challenges

Where procurement typically loses control, value or management visibility.

Project budgets are eroded by uncontrolled commitments

Site teams may place orders or agree subcontract changes before formal approval. Without commitment visibility, Finance sees the cost only when invoices arrive and project margin is already damaged.

Subcontractor qualification is high-risk

Subcontractors affect safety, quality, schedule, compliance and client satisfaction. Procurement must manage insurance, licenses, references, HSE evidence, capacity, financial risk and contract status before work starts.

Change orders and variations need evidence

Scope changes, site instructions and client-driven changes can become disputes when approval, pricing, photographs, delivery records or correspondence are missing. Procurement workflows must capture evidence at the moment of change.

Material price volatility affects margin

Steel, concrete, timber, MEP components, fuel and logistics costs can move during a project. Procurement must monitor validity dates, escalation clauses, framework terms and alternatives.

Site purchasing often bypasses central control

Urgent site needs, small tools, rental equipment and local materials can be bought outside preferred channels. This creates price leakage, duplicate suppliers and weak invoice matching.

Claims and retention require contract discipline

Payment milestones, retention, liquidated damages, warranties, defects liability and claim notices must be visible. Poor contract control weakens recovery and dispute position.
