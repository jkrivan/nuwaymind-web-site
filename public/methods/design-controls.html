Purpose

Design controls that make procurement policy executable in daily decisions.

NuWayMind converts diagnostic findings into decision rights, thresholds, process gates, workflow rules, evidence requirements and reporting logic. The objective is not to add bureaucracy; the objective is to ensure the right control is applied at the right point based on value, category risk, supplier criticality, contract exposure and business impact.

Planning assumption for lead-time estimates: company with approximately 5,000 employees, several purchasing populations, multi-level approval needs, mixed direct and indirect spend, multiple supplier risk classes and a need to align Procurement, Finance, Legal, IT, Quality/Compliance and business owners.

Risk-based control

Controls are designed around risk, value and supplier criticality, not only around approval hierarchy.

Minimum viable governance

The model keeps controls strong but avoids unnecessary approval layers that slow execution.

Workflow-ready rules

Every policy rule is written so it can be configured, monitored, evidenced and trained.

Standard KPIs

Control KPIs that prove whether governance is used correctly.

Standard

Approval SLA adherence

Formula: approvals completed within defined SLA divided by total approvals.

Why it matters: prevents control design from becoming an operational bottleneck.

Standard

Exception rate

Formula: transactions routed through exception logic divided by total controlled transactions.

Why it matters: measures whether business rules are realistic or frequently bypassed.

Standard

Control coverage

Formula: process steps with defined owner, rule, evidence and KPI divided by target process steps.

Why it matters: ensures the operating model covers the full source-to-pay chain.

Standard

Category owner coverage

Formula: addressable spend categories with appointed owners divided by target categories.

Why it matters: shifts procurement from reactive ordering to active spend stewardship.

Standard

Supplier risk classification coverage

Formula: active suppliers with assigned risk tier divided by target supplier population.

Why it matters: enables proportional controls for critical suppliers and simpler routing for low-risk suppliers.

Standard

Evidence completion

Formula: transactions with required documents, approvals and decision reasons divided by transactions sampled.

Why it matters: measures audit readiness before external or internal review.

Advanced KPIs

Advanced control KPIs used to balance discipline with speed.

Advanced

Risk-adjusted approval load

Formula: number of approval steps weighted by risk and value class.

Use: identifies excessive approvals for low-risk spend and insufficient control for high-risk spend.

Advanced

Control friction index

Formula: cycle-time delay, rework, rejected submissions and escalations weighted by process volume.

Use: shows whether control design is slowing the business more than necessary.

Advanced

Prevented commitment risk

Formula: value of transactions stopped, corrected or escalated before supplier commitment due to missing control conditions.

Use: quantifies the protection value of approvals and evidence gates.

Advanced

Policy-to-system execution ratio

Formula: policy requirements technically embedded in workflow divided by total policy requirements.

Use: reduces the gap between written policy and actual user behaviour.

Advanced

Segmentation accuracy

Formula: supplier or category risk classifications validated as correct during sample review divided by classifications sampled.

Use: prevents wrong routing caused by poor supplier or category data.

Advanced

Control resilience score

Formula: weighted score covering owner backup, delegation, exception rules, evidence availability and reporting continuity.

Use: tests whether controls still work during absence, urgency, entity changes or system disruption.
